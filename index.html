Aura Browser Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · App: Aura Browser (com.gr4nt.aurabrowser)

Aura Browser is a web browser for Android. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: we do not collect your data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no servers of its own.

Information we collect

None. The developer does not receive, store or sell any personal information from the app.

Information stored on your device

To work as a browser, Aura Browser keeps the following on your device only:

  • Open tabs, browsing history and bookmarks
  • Cookies, site data and cache created by the websites you visit
  • Files and videos you choose to download, and the download list
  • Your settings

This data never leaves your device through Aura Browser. You can clear it from within the app or in Android Settings, and uninstalling the app removes it.

Websites you visit

When you open a website, your device connects to that site and the site receives the information any browser sends (such as your IP address and browser details). The sites you visit, and the content on them, are governed by their own privacy policies. Aura Browser uses the Android System WebView (Chromium) to display pages.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load pages and downloads.
  • Camera, microphone and location: used only when a website asks for them and you allow it in the prompt. Aura Browser does not use them on its own and does not send them to the developer.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to show download progress and keep downloads running while the screen is off.
  • Wake lock and audio settings: to keep downloads and media playback running.

Ad and pop-up blocking

Blocking rules are built into the app and work on your device. Your browsing is not sent anywhere to decide what to block.

Children

Aura Browser gives access to the open web and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/Gr4nt-cell/Aura-Browser/issues.
